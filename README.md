# Indian_Stock_Market_1995_to_2024-Analysis

📊 Indian Stock Market Analysis (1995–2024)
1️⃣ Project Title

Indian Stock Market Analysis Dashboard (1995–2024)

2️⃣ Project Description

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of the Indian stock market from 1995 to 2024 using an interactive Power BI dashboard. The dashboard provides insights into stock listings, sector performance, market distribution, and listing trends over time.

The objective of this project is to analyze the growth of the Indian equity market, understand sector-wise distribution of companies, and identify listing patterns across years and months. The dashboard allows users to explore the data interactively through filters such as listing year, industry name, and company name.

This project demonstrates how data visualization and business intelligence tools can be used to transform raw stock market data into meaningful insights for better understanding of market trends.

3️⃣ 🛠 Tech Stack

The following tools and technologies were used to build this project:

Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation

Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preprocessing

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Creating calculated measures and KPIs

Power Query – Data transformation and preparation

4️⃣ 📂 Data Source

The dataset used for this project was obtained from:

Kaggle
It contains information related to Indian stock market companies, sectors, listings, and trading categories.

5️⃣ 📊 Features & Highlights
📌 Business Problem

The Indian stock market contains thousands of listed companies across multiple sectors and exchanges. Understanding how the market has evolved over time, which sectors dominate listings, and how companies are distributed across market categories can be challenging without proper visualization.

This project aims to simplify this information through an interactive dashboard that highlights market structure, sector leadership, and listing activity trends.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze the growth of stock listings over time

Understand sector-wise distribution of companies

Identify listing trends across years and months

Analyze market series and quality groups

Provide an interactive dashboard for better exploration of stock market data

📈 Key Dashboard Metrics

The dashboard provides high-level KPIs such as:

Total NSE Listings: ~1.97K companies

Total BSE Listings: ~3.94K companies

F&O Stocks: 194

Market Sectors: 12

These KPIs give a quick overview of the size and structure of the Indian stock market.

📊 Key Visualizations

The dashboard includes several important visualizations:

Sector Leaderboard

Shows the top sectors with the highest number of listed companies, such as:

Consumer Discretionary

Industrials

Commodities

Financial Services

Healthcare

Information Technology

This helps identify dominant sectors in the Indian market.

Listing Activity Trend

A time-series visualization that shows how stock listings have changed over the years.
It highlights periods of high and low listing activity, indicating market expansion phases.

Market Series Distribution

This visualization categorizes companies based on market trading series such as EQ, BE, and BZ, showing how companies are distributed across trading categories.

Market Quality Groups

A donut chart representing the distribution of companies across market quality groups (X, B, A, XT, M, T, Z).

This helps analyze market segmentation and classification of companies.

Monthly Listing Timeline

A bar chart showing monthly listing patterns, helping identify months with higher listing activity.

🔎 Insights

Some key insights from the dashboard include:

The Consumer Discretionary sector has the highest number of listed companies.

The Indian stock market has experienced fluctuating listing activity over the years, reflecting economic cycles.

Most companies belong to the EQ market series, indicating active trading classification.

The market contains multiple quality groups, highlighting the diversity in company classifications.
